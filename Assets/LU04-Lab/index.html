Savory Snacks

Written by Sam Smith - April 3rd, 2023

The hummus recipe was passed down from my great-great-great- grandfather. Family tradition held that it was only be passed down orally, and never written down. The purpose was to protect it from non-hummus lovers. I am the first to break that tradition.

The secret to a good hummus is cooking the chickpeas until they are extra soft. This will allow the hummus to become ultra smooth and less gritty. As my late grandfather always said to me, "Sam, stop being such a gritty pile of hummus."

Ingridients

  • Cooked Chickpeas
  • Tahini
  • Lemon Juice
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Paprika
